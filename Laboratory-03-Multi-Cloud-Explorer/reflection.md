# Mission Reflection

After completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was AWS. I was impressed by the wide range of services available through AWS, from computing and storage to databases, networking, security, and serverless technologies. I also found its scalability useful because organizations can start with small resources and increase their infrastructure as their workloads grow. This showed me why AWS can be suitable for both startups and large enterprises.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform have many similarities. All three provide core cloud services such as virtual machines, object storage, networking, databases, and identity management. They also support scalable infrastructure, pay-as-you-go pricing, multiple global regions, containers, and Kubernetes. Although their service names and specific features are different, the three platforms provide similar fundamental capabilities for building and running applications in the cloud.

Business requirements play an important role when selecting a cloud platform. During the client analysis, the university was better suited to Azure because it already used Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory. In comparison, the AI research company was better suited to GCP because of its strong AI and machine learning capabilities, including access to GPUs, TPUs, Vertex AI, and Kubernetes services. This demonstrated that the best cloud platform depends on the organization's existing technology, workload, budget, and goals rather than simply choosing the most popular provider.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that recommending a cloud platform is not about choosing a personal favorite. Instead, the consultant must understand the client's requirements and match them with the technical capabilities, cost, scalability, security, and services offered by each provider.

Finally, this mission improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio by giving me more experience with structured Markdown documentation, comparison tables, research files, and screenshots as technical evidence. I also learned how to organize multiple related files into a clear project structure, making my portfolio more professional and easier to review.
